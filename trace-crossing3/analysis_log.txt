# Crossing trace at directory depth 3: what the wings reach, and how rare the joins are

Footprint of a constant = the set of Mathlib directories at depth 3 (for example Mathlib/Analysis/SpecialFunctions) that its cone reaches through the library boundary. For a joint, the wings are its largest dominated children (up to six); a directory is exclusive to a wing when no other wing reaches it; the joint's crossings are the pairs of exclusive directories from different wings, each scored by how often Mathlib theorems depend on that pair together, as a ratio to what independence would give (1 = as often as chance, below 1 = rarer, 0 = never seen together). A rare pair is one with ratio below 0.5; a zero pair has never been seen together in Mathlib.
