The proof of the Riemann Hypothesis, as a tree of what depends on what

Every box is a Lean theorem that the rest of the proof passes through; its height is the share of the proof's 10.66 million declaration lines that exist only to prove it, and boxes to its right are the theorems it passes through in turn. The whole proof sits under one theorem, that no Dirichlet L-function vanishes right of the critical line. The 64 lines that get from there to the Riemann Hypothesis are too thin to draw.
